About Me

I will be a Postdoc Associate at SMART Centre, MIT under the supervision of Prof. Alexandre Jacquillat (MIT Sloan), Prof. Hai Wang (SMU), and Prof. Bo Lin (NUS).

I obtained my Ph.D. degree from the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics (IORA) at National University of Singapore (NUS). My advisors are Prof. Yifan Feng and Prof. Xiaobo Li.

I am currently visiting Prof. Xuan Wang in the ISOM Department at HKUST from December 2025 to August 2026.

I am committed to data-driven, practice-oriented, and interdisciplinary research, particularly at the intersection of OR and AI.
